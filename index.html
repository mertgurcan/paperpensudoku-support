Paper, Pen, Sudoku!

A clean and minimal Sudoku experience for iOS

Paper, Pen, Sudoku! is a distraction-free Sudoku app designed to feel like solving a puzzle on paper. No timers forcing you, no cluttered interface — just you and the puzzle.

Download on the App Store

What's inside

  • Three difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard
  • Light and dark mode
  • No account, no sign-up, no ads
  • Clean, paper-inspired design
  • Play offline once a puzzle is loaded

Privacy

Paper, Pen, Sudoku! does not collect personal data. Your progress stays on your device.
